A soft, romantic look perfect for wedding videography. This LUT enhances skin tones while maintaining a dreamy, soft aesthetic that flatters any lighting condition.

## Features

- **Soft Highlight Roll-off**: Natural, non-harsh highlights
- **Warm Skin Tones**: Enhances complexion without looking artificial
- **Subtle Saturation**: Rich colors without oversaturation
- **Low Contrast Style**: Perfect for bright outdoor ceremonies

## Compatible Footage

- Natural light weddings
- Indoor venue recordings
- Golden hour sequences
- First look sessions

- **Color Space**: REC.709 / sRGB
