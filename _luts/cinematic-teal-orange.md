A classic cinematic look with teal shadows and orange highlights. This LUT is inspired by the popular color grading technique used in many Hollywood films to create contrast and visual interest.

## Features

- **Teal Shadows**: Pushes shadows toward cyan/teal for depth
- **Orange Highlights**: Lifts skin tones and highlights to warm orange
- **Film-like Contrast**: Adds rich contrast without crushing blacks
- **Versatile Application**: Works with most camera profiles

## Compatible Footage

- Log footage (Log-C, S-Log, V-Log, etc.)
- Flat profiles (Rec.709, Neutral, etc.)
- RAW footage with proper white balance

## Technical Details

- **Format**: .CUBE (3D LUT)
- **Size**: 33x33x33
- **Color Space**: REC.709 / sRGB
