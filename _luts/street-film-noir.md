High contrast black and white with deep shadows. Perfect for urban street photography and documentary style videography. This LUT transforms ordinary footage into something with a gritty, artistic edge.

## Features

- **Deep Blacks**: Rich, ink-like blacks for dramatic effect
- **High Contrast**: Punchy whites without clipping
- **Desaturated Midtones**: Creates that classic noir look
- **Subtle Grain**: Optional film grain overlay available

## Compatible Footage

- Urban environments
- Night street scenes
- Documentary projects
- Interview backdrops

## Technical Details

- **Format**: .CUBE (3D LUT)
- **Size**: 33x33x33
- **Color Space**: REC.709 / sRGB
